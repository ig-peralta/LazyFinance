LazyFinance brings balances, spending, and goals into one clear conversation—so you can spend less time sorting data and more time deciding what matters.

Ask like a person

“How much did I spend on food this month?” Get a useful answer without digging through every transaction.

Notice what changed

Compare months, spot unusual charges, and catch duplicate purchases or subscription price changes.

Set a budget or a savings goal, then explore practical changes based on the way you already spend.
