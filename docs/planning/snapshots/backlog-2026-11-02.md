The team maintains issue descriptions, decisions, statuses, and ownership in Linear; this document reflects what was recorded when queried. **How to read the inventory:** Each line gives the issue ID, title, status, and due date where set. Child items are indented under their parent. Priorities and assignees are **unset unless explicitly noted**; absence of an assignee is not evidence about who contributed. Due dates are planning targets, not completion dates. The snapshot does not reconstruct which issues were selected at the beginning of Stage A or Stage B.

Each issue also has an individual **Done when** check distilled from its recorded outcome and acceptance criteria. For issues already marked Done, this describes the documented scope of the delivered slice, **not** a new checklist retroactively applied to past work. For Todo issues, it describes the outcome that still needs verification.
